# 3D Printer Companion privacy policy

_Last updated 29 October 2026_

3D Printer Companion is built to keep your data on your device. **We don't collect any data.**

## What the app stores, and where

- **Your account.** Your name and email, whether you sign up in the app or with Sign in with Apple, are kept on your iPhone or iPad only. They are never sent to us or anyone else.
- **Your library.** Models you import, the prints you slice and your print history are stored on your device.
- **Printer details.** The addresses of your printers, and for Bambu Lab printers the access code (kept in the device's Keychain), stay on your device.

## Who the app talks to

The app connects only to the printers you add: directly over your local network, or through a VPN you set up yourself. It doesn't contact any server run by us, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Sign in with Apple is handled by Apple; Apple shares only the name and email you choose with the app, and they stay on your device.

## Notifications

Notifications about your prints are created on your device. No push service run by us is involved.

## Deleting your data

Settings → Delete account removes your account and library from the device. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

## Children

The app isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at https://github.com/xLuciferSx/3d-printer-companion/issues.
