# 3D Printer Companion support

## Getting help

Open an issue at **https://github.com/xLuciferSx/3d-printer-companion/issues/new** and describe what happened, which printer you have,. We reply to every issue.

## Common questions

**The app can't find my printer.**
The printer must be switched on and connected to the same Wi-Fi as your iPhone, and 3D Printer Companion needs Local Network access: Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network. You can also add a printer by its address in the app's Settings tab; ELEGOO printers show their address under Settings → Network on the printer's screen.

**Can I use it away from home?**
The app connects directly to your printer's address. If you have your own way of reaching your home network from outside, add the printer by the address it has there. We don't recommend opening a printer's ports to the internet: printers have no password.

**Which printers work?**
ELEGOO Centauri Carbon (with or without CANVAS), Klipper printers with Moonraker (Anycubic Kobra with Rinkhals, Creality K1/K2, Voron, Sovol and others), and Bambu Lab X1, P1 and A1 in LAN mode.

**I don't have a printer yet. Can I try it?**
Yes. In the Printer tab, tap **Try the demo printer**. It's a printer simulated inside the app, with a print already running.

**How do I delete my account?**
Settings → Delete account. Your account and library live only on your device, so this removes them completely.
