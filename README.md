# 3D Printer Companion

Watch, slice and print on your 3D printer from your iPhone or iPad: live progress, temperatures and camera, 3D views of every model and print, a slicer for STL, 3MF and OBJ files, and notifications when a print finishes or needs you.

Works with ELEGOO Centauri Carbon (including the CANVAS multicolour system), Klipper printers running Moonraker, and Bambu Lab printers in LAN mode.
